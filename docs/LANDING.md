## 4. Design rules

- Dark: `<html data-theme="dark">`, canvas `#000`; the one exception is the hero, a light panel (§5.1). Use the token variables from `tokens.css`; hex values below are for orientation.
- Text: primary `#FFFFFF`, secondary `#AEAEB2`, tertiary `#86868B`, quiet `#6E6E73`. Signal red `#FF3B30`, eyebrow red `#FF6555`, soft red text `#FF9D8E`. Status: green `#30D158`, yellow `#FFD60A`, blue `#0A84FF`, gray `#8E8E93`.
- Fonts: Rota's stack for sans (SF on Apple devices), `"Inter"` before `system-ui` elsewhere. Never ship SF Pro files (Apple's license). Geist Mono for eyebrows, numbers, codes (`М-02`, `№164`).
- Type, desktop: H1 72/1.0 bold, tracking −0.035em. Centered section H2 72/1.02, side-by-side chapter H2 56/1.04, both −0.03em. Lead 19/1.55 secondary. Body 17/1.5. Eyebrow Geist Mono 500, 12 px, uppercase, tracking 0.16em, eyebrow red. Mobile: H1 44, H2 38, lead 17.
- Layout: content max 1200 px, side padding 120 → 20 on mobile; section padding 120 px top and bottom (72 on mobile). Side-by-side chapters: 2 columns 1fr 1fr, gap 80, stack under 1024 px.
- Glow: a blurred red ellipse behind each section's visual (`radial-gradient(closest-side, rgba(255,59,48,.30), transparent)` 700 × 560 px), as in the references.
- Chapter number: outlined numeral behind the visual, Geist Mono or SF bold 400 px, `-webkit-text-stroke: 2px rgba(255,255,255,.06)`, transparent fill (ref `rota-02-story.png`, `rota-07-quiet.png`).
- Glass card: `rgba(255,255,255,.05)` background, 1 px `rgba(255,255,255,.12)` border, radius 28–32, `backdrop-filter: blur(24px) saturate(140%)`.
- Pills: glass pill (`.06` fill, `.14` border, radius 999, 10 × 16 padding) with a 8–10 px status dot.
- Buttons (Rota `Button`): primary = white pill, black text, 52 px tall; glass = translucent pill, white text. Focus ring 2 px `#FF6555`, offset 2.
- Mascots: the `Mascot` component by pose name, 160–320 px, at most one per section (two in the final CTA), gentle float (6 px, 4 s, ease-in-out).
- Motion: `Reveal` fades elements up (opacity 0 → 1, translateY 16 → 0, 500 ms, stagger 60 ms) once. No scroll-jacking, no parallax libraries. Total JS ≤ 150 KB gzip.
